Boring books, done right

Zero2One is a small, founder-led accounting firm for ecommerce brands on Shopify and Amazon. We keep your books reconciled every month and explain them in plain language — no jargon, no surprises at month-end.

Why Zero2One exists

Most ecommerce founders don't need a flashy "finance platform." They need their books done properly, on time, every month — by someone who understands how Shopify payouts, processor fees, refunds, and Amazon settlements actually flow.

That's what Zero2One does. We set your books up the way online sellers need them — A2X for Shopify and Amazon, a clean chart of accounts, and COGS tracked properly — then keep them reconciled and send you a short report you'll actually read.

We keep our client list small on purpose, so the work stays careful and you deal with the person actually doing it.
