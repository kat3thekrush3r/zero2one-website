Data & Accounting Cleanup

We ensure that every transaction is categorized correctly in your data warehouse and QBO and we put the processes in place necessary to make sure it stays clean and your financials can truly be relied upon. No surprises, no guesswork—just financials you can actually trust.

Data Engineering

We completely automate the process of pulling your data from all of your data sources—Shopify, Amazon, ad platforms, 3PLs—refreshing hourly and making it available for use in your own Big Query data warehouse. Available to you and your team whenever you need it.

Accounting

Our obsessive focus on ecommerce has enabled us to streamline the accounting process to the point that it is possible to get true insight into your profitability on a daily basis. Full CPA-designed 3-statement financial close process within 5 days of month-end—on time, every time. Because you shouldn't have to wait until mid-February to know how January went.

FP&A

Know your ROAS by the hour when every hour counts during critical sales events like Black Friday or Amazon Prime Days. We equip your team with every metric necessary for success—LTV, CAC, contribution margin, AOV, product mix, COGS and more—timely enough to make a real difference.

How We Work

We're not your typical accounting firm. Here's what makes us different.

We ruthlessly eliminate busywork

If a machine can do it, a human shouldn't. We use AI, automation, and smart integrations to eliminate every manual, repetitive task from your finance operations. No more data entry. No more reconciliation hell. Just clean, automated workflows that run in the background while you focus on growing your business.

We take full ownership

If it has a dollar sign attached to it, we're responsible. We don't do the "that's not my department" thing. We work with a limited number of clients so we can be fully embedded in your business—responding in real-time, thinking proactively, and operating like we're part of your team. Because we are.

Cash drives every decision

Fancy dashboards are nice. Smooth automation feels good. But neither matters if they're not making you money. We prioritize work based on one question: Will this increase profitability? The answer guides everything we build, every system we improve, and every recommendation we make.
