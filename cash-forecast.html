The problem

Why "13 weeks" is the wrong unit.

The 13-week model was built for lenders and turnarounds — not for a fast-moving ecommerce brand timing inventory buys against marketplace payouts.

The 13-week forecast

One number per week.

  • Averages a whole week into one figure — you can look fine on Friday and be overdrawn on Wednesday.
  • Usually built once and left to rot — stale within days of a big payout or supplier payment.
  • Too coarse to time a $30k inventory PO, a payroll run, or a sales-tax payment.
  • Tells you that you'll be tight — never which day it actually bites.
The daily cash model

A balance for every day.

  • See the exact date cash dips — and exactly how far below your buffer it goes.
  • Schedule supplier payments, payroll, and tax around real payout timing.
  • Know how hard you can push ad spend this week without breaking cash.
  • Driven by real payouts and bills, updated as the week unfolds — not a quarterly guess.

How the template works

Open it in Excel or Google Sheets and start on today. It's deliberately simple — the power is in the daily resolution, not in complexity.
