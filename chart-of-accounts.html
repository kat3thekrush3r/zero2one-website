Why it matters

If your chart of accounts is wrong, every report after it is too.

It's not that ecommerce founders are bad with numbers. It's that a generic, out-of-the-box chart of accounts was never built for how you actually sell.

Payouts ≠ revenue

When a Shopify or Amazon deposit hits and gets booked as "sales," your revenue is understated and your fees disappear. Clearing accounts fix that.

No real gross margin

If product cost, freight, and platform fees aren't in COGS, you can't see contribution margin — so you can't price or scale ads with confidence.

Inventory in the dark

Stock in transit, at the 3PL, and in FBA needs its own homes. Lump it together and your balance sheet stops meaning anything.

Surprises at tax time

Sales tax collected, owner draws, and financing tangled into the P&L turn year-end into a cleanup project. A clean structure keeps them separate from day one.
