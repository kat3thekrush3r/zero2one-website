What affects your price

We quote one flat monthly fee. Three things move it up or down — and we walk you through all three before you sign anything.

Transaction volume

More orders, payouts, and expenses mean more to reconcile each month. Lower volume sits in Starter; higher volume moves toward Scale.

Sales channels

One store is simpler than Shopify plus Amazon plus a marketplace. Each channel adds settlements and fees we need to map correctly.

Inventory & COGS complexity

Tracking cost of goods and inventory by SKU takes more work than category-level totals. The more detail you need, the higher the tier.

No hourly billing and no surprise invoices — you pay the same agreed amount every month, and we tell you in advance if your workload changes enough to revisit it.
